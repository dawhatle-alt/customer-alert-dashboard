Root cause accuracy by TSA
Control-M cases closed in the last 30 days, by case owner. Jev reads each case's description, last emails and case notes (but not the recorded root cause) and picks RC1 and RC2 using the Root Cause Best Practices guide, including its left-to-right order: Install/Upgrade/Rehost/Migration → Product Issue → Environment → Product Usage → Void. A recorded category "matches" when Jev rates it at least 60% as likely as its own top pick. The guide's rules are checked directly: Void needs Disposition Void and no RC2, blank RC1/RC2, and categories outside the guide. Mainframe products (Control-M for z/OS, IOA, INCONTROL, Control-D, Control-O, Tape, Analyzer) still use the older RC tree, which the guide does not cover, so those cases are listed as "Not covered by guide" and left out of accuracy and ranking, as are cases Jev judges too thin to assess. RC2 values outside the guide's list are not scored. Click a TSA to see each case.
